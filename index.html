Our Story

Mayorie Technologies Limited was founded in 2021 with a simple yet powerful vision: to democratize access to quality technology education worldwide.

Our founders recognized a critical gap in the education system talented individuals eager to learn tech skills but lacking access to experienced mentors who could provide personalized guidance and real-world insights.

What started as a small platform connecting a handful of students with local mentors has grown into a global community spanning over 50 countries. Today, we partner with 200+ expert mentors who have trained more than 5,000 students in cutting-edge digital skills.

Our success is measured not just in numbers, but in the lives we've transformed— students who've switched careers, launched startups, secured dream jobs, and become mentors themselves, continuing the cycle of knowledge sharing.

We're just getting started. Join us in building the future of technology education.
